Reading order

Brandon’s books in the Prince Martin Epic series can be read in order or as stand-alones. The series grows with your son: each successive book gets longer and the plot more action-packed!

  1. Book 1 · Ages 6+

    Prince Martin Wins His Sword

    Can a boy prince win his sword by proving he’s Brave, Loyal, and True in a crisis? Book 1 of the Prince Martin Epic, an illustrated rhyming adventure series by Brandon Hale. Ages 6+.

  2. Book 2 · Ages 7+

    Prince Martin and the Thieves

    A boy prince and his canine sidekick are caught and held for ransom by ruthless robbers! Do past good deeds pay dividends? Could help come from where it’s least expected? Book 2 of the Prince Martin Epic, an illustrated rhyming adventure series by Brandon Hale. Ages 7+.

  3. Book 3 · Ages 7+

    Prince Martin and the Dragons

    Can a boy prince and his pals rid the realm of devastating dragons? Book 3 of the Prince Martin Epic, an illustrated rhyming adventure series by Brandon Hale. Ages 7+.

  4. Book 4 · Ages 8+

    Prince Martin and the Cave Bear

    Can Prince Martin rescue a blind cave bear kidnapped by a cruel Northman? Book 4 of the Prince Martin Epic, an illustrated rhyming adventure series by Brandon Hale. Ages 8+.

  5. Book 5 · Ages 8+

    Prince Martin and the Last Centaur

    Can Prince Martin persuade an ancient war hero to fight one last time to rescue the wilderness from the ruthless Marauders gang? Book 5 of the Prince Martin Epic, an illustrated rhyming adventure series by Brandon Hale. Ages 8+.

  6. Book 6 · Ages 9+

    Prince Martin and the Pirates

    When Prince Martin is captured by the Dread Pirate Kaine and his perfidious crew, the kid discovers that his only hope might come from the sea itself... Book 6 of the Prince Martin Epic, an illustrated rhyming adventure series by Brandon Hale. Ages 9+.

  7. Book 7 · Ages 10+

    Prince Martin and the Quest for the Bloodstone

    Pirates on the prowl. A prince on a mission. Book 7 of the Prince Martin Epic, an illustrated rhyming adventure series by Brandon Hale. Ages 10+.

Is your son a reluctant reader?

The Prince Martin Epic series is a perfect fit because each book features:

  • High-stakes action-adventure and danger
  • Brilliant color illustrations that burst off the page
  • Text that appears to be prose—but subtly rhymes, gently nudging the boy along as he is encouraged to find the next rhyme

This is the perfect formula for engaging boy readers.

The Prince Martin Epic series is adventure books that fill a need: because they RHYME your son will read!

This book series will hook your son on reading because the subtly rhyming text fascinates reluctant readers, who can’t wait to discover the next epic rhyme. Your son will also love the books because of the explosive art and high-stakes action-adventure. These are not “safe” books!

Parents will appreciate these classic tales because they build character, model virtue, and inspire grit and resilience in boys. The series grows with your son: Each successive book gets longer and the plot more action-packed! And each book can be read as a stand-alone.

Introduce your son to Prince Martin today because boys need virtuous heroes!
