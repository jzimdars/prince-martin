When the King is injured on a journey, can Little Prince Martin save him?

On a grand expedition to the manor home of Sir Godfrey the Knight, misadventure strikes! Now it’s up to Little Prince Martin to save his father, the King!

Little boys who love big adventure will adore this heartwarming and exciting tale featuring brilliant illustrations and playful rhyming verse.

This unforgettable picture book is the third of three Little Prince Martin prequels to the Prince Martin Epic chapter book series.
