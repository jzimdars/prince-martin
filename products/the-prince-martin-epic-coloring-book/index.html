The Prince Martin Epic Coloring Book

By Brandon Hale · Illustrated by Jason Zimdars

Action-packed scenes from the virtue-building adventure series about castles and quests, knights and knaves

This collection of art is a book for the ages:
It’s bursting with action-packed coloring pages!

In an age when young boys had been hero-deprived,
The brave tales of Prince Martin, thank heaven, arrived.

Now the boys of this realm are no longer forsaken!
From that series of books, this collection is taken.

Boys of various ages can color with ardor:
Some pages are easy and others are harder.

Compare page count with price…and you’ll make the connection:
You get bang for your buck with this beefy collection!

Buy this book for a red-blooded, big-hearted boy—
Watch him color with wonder, enchantment, and joy!
