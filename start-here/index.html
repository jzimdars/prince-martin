New to Prince Martin?

Where do I start?

Pick the series that fits your child’s age, then start with Book 1.

Ages 3–7

Little Prince Martin picture books

Short, playful rhyming picture books for younger readers, and a prequel to the Epic. Start with Honesty Pays.

  1. Book 1 · Ages 3–7 · 32 pages Honesty Pays: A Little Prince Martin Picture Book
  2. Book 2 · Ages 3–7 · 34 pages Little Prince Martin and the Seven Labors: Small Boys Can Do Big Jobs
  3. Book 3 · Ages 3–7 · 34 pages Little Prince Martin Braves the Night—to Save the Day

About the picture books →

Ages 6+ to 10+

The Prince Martin Epic

Seven illustrated chapter books in subtly rhyming prose. Start with Book 1, Prince Martin Wins His Sword. Each book gets longer and more action-packed than the last, so the series grows with your son, and each one can be read as a stand-alone.

  1. Book 1 · Ages 6+ · 55 pages Prince Martin Wins His Sword
  2. Book 2 · Ages 7+ · 93 pages Prince Martin and the Thieves
  3. Book 3 · Ages 7+ · 98 pages Prince Martin and the Dragons
  4. Book 4 · Ages 8+ · 172 pages Prince Martin and the Cave Bear
  5. Book 5 · Ages 8+ · 194 pages Prince Martin and the Last Centaur
  6. Book 6 · Ages 9+ · 163 pages Prince Martin and the Pirates
  7. Book 7 · Ages 10+ · 243 pages Prince Martin and the Quest for the Bloodstone

Ages 5–12

The Prince Martin Epic Coloring Book

170 pages of action-packed scenes from the Epic to color, some easy and some harder. A good companion to any of the books.

  1. Ages 5–12 · 170 pages The Prince Martin Epic Coloring Book
